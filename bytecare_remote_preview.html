FAQ

Questions before booking

Can every PC problem be fixed remotely?

No. Remote support is best for software, configuration and diagnosis. Physical hardware faults, broken screens and component replacement require in-person service.

Do you connect without my permission?

No. A remote session should only start after you approve it. You can end the session whenever you want.

Is a fix guaranteed?

No. The cause may require hardware service, licensed software, or further testing. The scope and fee are discussed before work begins.

Do I need to install a remote support app?

Depending on your device and the support method, you may need to use a trusted remote-support tool. Only use it after you have verified the support provider.
